Privacy Policy

App: Learn Myanmar Alphabet  |  Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: the app has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics and no in-app purchases. We do not collect or receive any personal information from you or your child. Learning progress stays on the device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and Unity Ads, which are set up for children (non-personalised, child-directed) and do not use the advertising ID.

1. Who we are

“Learn Myanmar Alphabet” (the “app”) is published by Ru Di Pah (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles information. You can contact us at learnmyanmaralphabet@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

The app does not ask for, collect, store on our servers or share:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers or any account or login details;
  • precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data;
  • an advertising identifier. The app does not ask permission to track you and does not use the advertising ID to follow children across apps or websites.

We do not run any analytics or tracking tools of our own, and we have no servers that receive data from the app.

3. Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves a few things only on your device: which letters have been practised and how often, a daily-study streak, and settings such as sound on/off and the audio style. This information is never sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app, or you can clear it at any time in Settings → Reset All Progress.

4. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads using two advertising services:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) – policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) – unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Because the app is designed for children, we configure these services for child-directed use:

  • ads are non-personalised: no interest-based advertising, no behavioural profiling and no remarketing;
  • ad content is limited to a family-friendly rating, and we do not request the advertising ID;
  • the app is marked as directed to children in these services’ settings.

To deliver ads, limit how often an ad is shown, prevent fraud and measure ad performance, these services may automatically process limited technical information such as the device’s IP address, device type, operating system and app version, and whether an ad was viewed or tapped. This is handled by those companies under their own privacy policies; we do not receive it. Ads may contain links that open a website outside the app, so we recommend that a parent or guardian supervises young children.

5. Children’s privacy

This app is designed for children, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app contains no social features, chat, user-generated content, in-app purchases or links to other services other than through advertisements. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question or concern about your child’s use of the app, please contact us and we will respond promptly. Because we do not hold personal information about users, there is normally nothing for us to delete; for data handled by the advertising services above, please contact them using the links in section 4.

6. Data retention and security

We do not store personal information on any server. Information kept on your device is protected by your device’s own security and remains under your control.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK or EU GDPR), you may have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction and deletion. As we do not collect or hold personal data from the app, these requests are normally not applicable to us, but you are welcome to ask us a question at any time.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, for example by adding a new service, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above before the change takes effect.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: learnmyanmaralphabet@gmail.com
