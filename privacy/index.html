Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Learn Myanmar Alphabet" mobile app ("the App") handles information. The App is designed for young children learning the Myanmar alphabet and numbers, and we are committed to protecting children's privacy in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

Information We Collect

The App does not require account creation, login, or collection of personal information such as name, email, or location directly from users.

The App may use third-party services that collect limited technical information automatically, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system version
  • App usage data (e.g., crash logs, screens viewed)

This data is collected only through the third-party services listed below, not directly by us.

Third-Party Services

Advertising — Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display ads. Because this App is directed at children, we configure AdMob to serve ads under Google's child-directed treatment settings, which restrict personalized advertising and limit data collection in accordance with COPPA. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Google Play Services

The App relies on Google Play Services to function on Android devices, which may collect basic device and diagnostic information as described in Google's own Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

This App is directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information beyond what is disclosed above, and any advertising shown is served under child-directed, non-personalized ad settings. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided personal information to us can contact us using the details below to request deletion.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Any data collected by third-party services (such as AdMob) is governed by that provider's own privacy policy and is used only for the purposes described above.

Parental Rights

Parents and guardians can contact us to:

  • Ask questions about our data practices
  • Request review or deletion of any data associated with their child

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
